# Sell The Method — Reference Recreation Direction

## Ground-Truth Reference

The design faithfully recreates the visual hierarchy, section sequence, persuasive sales-page structure, and interaction language of the supplied `sellthemethod.com` reference. Fidelity to the reference is the guiding design constraint; the only intentional visual departure is the client-supplied **deep black and emerald** color system.

### Reference Structure to Preserve

The page is a long-form, editorial sales landing page composed of a constellation-grid hero; a dark browser-window-style proof panel; proof and case-study image galleries; student result panels; two stacked pricing offers; an influencer proof strip; founder letter; real-time account timeline; vault preview and module list; comparative value statements; FAQs; and a concise footer. It uses generous vertical rhythm, sharp rectangular framing, lowercase/uppercase micro-labels, high-contrast serif display headlines, dense monospace metadata, and stacked evidence cards.

### Fidelity Principles

1. **Editorial directness:** The typography should feel assertive, simple, and proof-led rather than ornamental.
2. **Evidence as interface:** Sales proof, screenshots, and timelines are presented as tangible artifacts in an asymmetric collage rather than generic content blocks.
3. **Industrial precision:** Squared edges, hairline rules, browser chrome, dot grids, and monospaced labels provide the page's structural voice.
4. **Emerald restraint:** Emerald is reserved for high-intent actions, indicators, underlines, and signals, never applied as a broad gradient wash.

### Interaction and Motion

The navigation remains minimal and unobtrusive. CTAs respond with a tight press scale and emerald wash. FAQ rows expand with a short opacity and transform transition. In-view sections may enter with a subtle upward offset and cascading delay, with all non-essential animation disabled for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System

* **Display:** `DM Serif Display` for large editorial headlines, with strategic italic emphasis.
* **UI and body:** `Manrope` for clear transactional content and legible long-form writing.
* **Metadata:** `IBM Plex Mono` for counters, labels, pricing annotations, and browser chrome.

### Wordmark & Logo

A compact wordmark, **SELLTHEMETHOD**, is rendered as precisely tracked uppercase metadata. The graphic mark is a transparent, squared loop constructed from three ascending emerald line segments: a compact visual shorthand for a method becoming a system.

### Brand Essence

**A proof-led course-system landing page for independent creators who want to package knowledge into a sellable digital product.**

**Personality:** Exacting, candid, kinetic.
